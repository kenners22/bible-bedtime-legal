Bible Bedtime Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

Bible Bedtime shares Bible story videos and bedtime reflections through official social media channels. This policy explains how the TikTok developer integration is intended to handle data.

Information We Access

When the Bible Bedtime TikTok account owner authorizes the integration, TikTok may provide basic account information such as open ID, avatar, and display name, depending on approved scopes. This information is used only to confirm the authorized account and support publishing to the official Bible Bedtime TikTok profile.

Publishing Data

The integration may upload approved Bible Bedtime video files and associated posting metadata to TikTok through TikTok's Content Posting API. It is not used to access or publish to unrelated accounts.

Storage

OAuth tokens and configuration files are stored privately on the account owner's local system or approved automation environment. Secrets are not intentionally published or shared in public chat, documentation, or source files.

Sharing

Bible Bedtime does not sell personal information. Data is shared with TikTok only as necessary to authorize the account and publish approved Bible Bedtime content through TikTok's official APIs.

Retention

Authorization tokens and related records are retained only as long as needed to operate the Bible Bedtime publishing workflow. Access can be revoked through TikTok account or app settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Bible Bedtime through the official TikTok profile: https://www.tiktok.com/@biblebedtimestories22.
