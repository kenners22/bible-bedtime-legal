Bible Bedtime Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

Bible Bedtime shares Bible story videos and bedtime reflections through official social media channels. This policy explains what information may be accessed when the account owner connects an official publishing account.

Information We Access

When the Bible Bedtime account owner authorizes an official social platform connection, that platform may provide basic account information such as account ID, avatar, and display name, depending on approved permissions. This information is used only to confirm the authorized account and support publishing to the official Bible Bedtime profile.

Publishing Data

Approved Bible Bedtime video files and associated posting metadata may be sent to official social platforms for publishing. The workflow is not used to access or publish to unrelated accounts.

Storage

OAuth tokens and configuration files are stored privately on the account owner's local system or approved automation environment. Secrets are not intentionally published or shared in public chat, documentation, or source files.

Sharing

Bible Bedtime does not sell personal information. Data is shared with social platforms only as necessary to authorize the account and publish approved Bible Bedtime content.

Retention

Authorization tokens and related records are retained only as long as needed to operate the Bible Bedtime publishing workflow. Access can be revoked through TikTok account or app settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Bible Bedtime through the official TikTok profile: https://www.tiktok.com/@biblebedtimestories22.
